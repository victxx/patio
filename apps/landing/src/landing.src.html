The mempool

Where transactions wait.
Where Patio lives.

Every 12 seconds a block empties the pool. Patio's packets are built to stay behind.

How it works

Transactions become packets.

  1. 1

    Capture

    Mic or camera becomes a tiny WebM stream.

  2. 2

    Sign

    A temporary wallet signs every slice.

  3. 3

    Broadcast

    Packets wait in the mempool.

  4. 4

    Listen

    Listeners rebuild the stream.

  5. 5

    Close

    Leftover ETH comes back.
